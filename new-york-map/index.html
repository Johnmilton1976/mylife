The New York Map of MY LIFE

MY LIFE is a New York life simulator — and the city is the co-star. This Manhattan is not a photo and not a stock map: it is drawn entirely by code, street by street, building by building, every time you start a life. Zoom out and you see the whole island grid; zoom in and you are standing on the crosswalk of 14th & 1st, taxis streaming past. Nothing is downloaded — the city exists inside one HTML file.

A real Manhattan skeleton

The map keeps the bones of the real thing:

  • A numbered street grid — east-west streets crossed by numbered avenues, the same way Upper Manhattan actually lays out
  • Central Park — a full green corridor with its own paths; the blocks around it are the expensive ones, and the game prices reflect it
  • The Broadway diagonal — the one road that refuses the grid, slicing across it like the original
  • The rivers and the Brooklyn Bridge — the city is an island, and the bridge is on the route
  • Neighborhood flavor — Harlem markets, Chelsea food halls, SoHo shopping, Midtown towers; the building names change as you cross town

The city has a clock and a mood

Light moves with the in-game clock — day, dusk and night wash over the streets, windows light up at night, seasons turn and weather rolls through. The same corner looks different at 8 AM and 8 PM, and the difference is drawn, not faked. Yellow cabs cruise every lane the whole time; hover one and the driver hands you a line from Munger, Buffett or the Rich Dad.

Walking it

You don't free-roam — the route takes you past every street eventually, spin after spin (see how to play). Zoom out with the scroll wheel or pinch and the whole plan of the city snaps into view; that is also the fastest way to spot where your next job, bank or realty office actually sits. Your life happens street by street, and the 14 goals are pinned to buildings you will walk past a hundred times.
