PRIVACY POLICY

Short version: we do not know who you are, and we like it that way. No account, no sign-up, no advertising cookies, no data broker. This page explains the few things that do touch data.

1. Your game save

Your life — name, money, skills, position — is saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data erases it; we cannot recover it, and we cannot read it.

2. What we collect

The game page itself runs no tracking scripts and sets no cookies. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may count anonymous, aggregated page visits through its privacy-friendly Web Analytics feature — no cookies, no fingerprinting, no personal profiles. We can see things like “about 150 visits this week” and nothing more.

3. Payments (PRO upgrade)

If you buy the optional MY LIFE PRO upgrade, the payment is processed by Stripe, Inc. on their own checkout. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us. We receive only what Stripe shows the seller: your email address (for the receipt) and the payment status. We use it solely to deliver your unlock code and handle refunds. Stripe's own privacy policy governs its handling of your data.

4. Email you send us

If you write to us (support, refunds, feedback), we keep your message and reply from our mailbox. We do not add you to any list and we do not share your address.

5. Children

The game is family-friendly and collects no personal data by design. If a child buys the PRO upgrade, a parent can request a full refund within 14 days via the contact page.

6. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we store no personal data server-side, there is generally nothing to export or delete. For anything related to a payment, email us and we will handle it within 30 days.

7. Changes

If this policy ever changes materially, the “last updated” date above changes with it.
