The 14 Life Goals of MY LIFE

In MY LIFE, the free life simulator game, you are not chasing a high score — you are climbing a ladder of 14 life goals, from your first paycheck to the day money stops being the boss. The panel top-right counts them down (0 / 14 at birth); each goal is banked the moment its condition is true, in any order. Bank all 14 and the city prints the only ending there is: a closing card that reads A LIFE, LIVED. Here is every goal, the exact condition, and how to reach it.

01
Land your first job (E quadrant)

Condition: you are employed, anywhere. Tip: stop on any company building in your first week — even DELIVERY DRIVER at $3,800/month is seed money. Salary is where every other goal starts.

02
Buy a dividend-paying stock (I quadrant)

Condition: your dividend income is greater than zero. Tip: any share from Wall St Bank's floor. The ticker doesn't matter yet — the direction of cash flow does.

03
Own a rental with positive cash flow

Condition: net rent (rent minus costs) is positive every month. Tip: the cheapest duplexes clear the bar easiest. A rental that eats money is a liability with a roof — see the Rich Dad rules.

04
Reach LV3 in any skill

Condition: any of the 8 skills hits level 3. Tip: study what your job uses — tuition rises with level, but a LV3 skill unlocks the top of a job ladder worth triple the tuition.

05
Pay yourself first 3 months running

Condition: the 10% sweep survives three straight months. Tip: turn it on early and never touch the dial. The count resets if you break the streak — it's a habit the game audits.

06
Get HAPPY up to 80

Condition: happiness ≥ 80. Tip: restaurants, cinema, Union Square, the gym — fun is a budget line, not a sin. Burnout drags HAPPY down faster than any night out can lift it.

07
Survive 30 days in New York

Condition: day 30. Tip: the first month-end settles all bills at once. Keep a cash buffer above your monthly expenses or the city will eat the unprepared.

08
Passive income covers 25% of your bills

Condition: freedom meter ≥ 25%. Tip: dividends + net rent against your expense base. Cut the bills and the percentage climbs twice.

09
Use GOOD DEBT to buy a cash-flowing asset

Condition: the good-debt flag is set. Tip: borrow at 6% APR only against an asset that yields more — the asset pays the interest, you keep the spread. Bad debt at 15% never counts.

10
Own a business that runs without you (B quadrant)

Condition: you own a business. Tip: the B quadrant is the exit from trading hours for money — income that doesn't stop when you stop.

11
Hold #1 on the city rankings for 3 straight months

Condition: 3 consecutive months at the top of the weekly net-value board. Tip: the ranking is weekly and the neighbors play hard. Three unbroken months is a dynasty, not a lucky week.

12
Earn a reputation pay rise

Condition: reputation > 0. Tip: show up. Streaks of good work days and steady performance build a permanent pay premium nobody can fire out of you.

13
Post a perfect month at work (85+)

Condition: a month's average performance at 85 or above. Tip: don't stack work days to burnout — the body keeps the receipt, and an ER visit wrecks the average.

14
FINANCIAL FREEDOM · passive income covers every bill

Condition: freedom meter = 100%. Tip: the ladder runs 25% → 100%. Every dividend, every rent check, every business dollar moves it — until the bills can no longer reach your salary.

What happens at 14 / 14

The ending plays once: the city stops, and your life closes on a card with your name on it — the walk, the assets, the person your numbers say you were. The save is banked so you can replay a different life, or start another from 18 with $1,000 and nothing else. The rules you'll be playing by are the Rich Dad rules; the streets you'll be walking them on are on the New York map; and if any of this is your first hour in the city, start with how to play.
