BitLife Alternative

BitLife turned "live a whole life in your pocket" into a habit for millions. You tap through birth, school, jobs, marriage, and death, watching the stats tick up. It lives on phones. So if you are on a laptop, at a desk, or simply do not want another app on your phone, the natural question is: what is the BitLife alternative for a browser? This page is the straight answer — what those alternatives are, what to expect, and where MY LIFE sits among them.

Why people go looking for a BitLife alternative

  • No app store. Some people just do not want to install anything — no storage used, no permission prompts, no update nagging.
  • A bigger screen. A life sim is a wall of text and numbers; reading it on a laptop is easier than on a phone.
  • No account, no data trail. Many mobile games want an email, a password, and a profile. A browser game that saves locally asks for none of it.
  • Free without being interrupted. Ad-supported mobile titles charge attention for progress; a no-account browser game does not have to.

What a good browser alternative actually does

The label "life simulator" covers a lot. A worthwhile one, whichever form it takes, should do four things: run an entire lifespan from birth toward an ending; make money behave like money — a salary, rent, and assets that actually pay you; let decisions carry delayed consequences so a choice costs nothing now and something later; and save without uploading your data. If a game hits those four, the form barely matters.

Where MY LIFE fits

MY LIFE is not a BitLife clone. It is a browser hybrid: you spin, walk a Manhattan grid, and land on a building that is the choice in front of you. A right-hand ledger shows cash, salary, monthly cash flow, stress and health at all times. The point of the game is the Rich Dad loop — buy assets, let them pay you, cover your expenses, and bank the 18 goals. It is free, needs no install, asks for no account, and your save stays in your own browser. The rules page and the city map explain both halves.

Frequently asked

Is MY LIFE a BitLife alternative? If you want a game that plays a whole life from birth to death, yes. MY LIFE covers the same arc — school, jobs, money, relationships, health — but it is free, browser-based and has no ads or microtransactions.

How is it different from BitLife? BitLife is a phone app with in-app purchases. MY LIFE runs in a browser with no install and no paywalls, and it leans into the money lesson (assets vs liabilities) rather than randomness.

Can I play it on a laptop instead of a phone? Yes. Because it is a browser game, a laptop or tablet is the most comfortable place to play — no phone required.
