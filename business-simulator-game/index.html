Business Simulator Game

A good business simulator game is not about clicking "hire" until a number goes up — it is about the moment a company stops needing you. In MY LIFE, you start at 18 with a little cash and a city full of choices: take a job, learn a skill, buy assets, and eventually build ventures that pay you whether or not you show up. It is a free browser business simulator that folds entrepreneurship into a whole life, with no download and no account.

Notice the shape of the ladder: the Coffee Cart costs $1,500 with no skill gate and no failure risk, so a brand-new player can own something on day one. The profit formula, not a random number

Month-end profit is not a dice roll on a number. The game computes each venture's payout as base income × operator quality × skill mastery × business age, plus or minus the variance band. Operator quality is driven by your stress, health and mood in the daily loop — a burnt-out owner earns less from the exact same business. Skill mastery rewards having practiced the trade; business age rewards letting a venture mature. Stress also multiplies the failure roll: at high stress, even a low-risk venture is more likely to go under.

Worked example — a Bakery at $1,800 base income, owned by a calm, skilled operator, might clear close to $2,000 in a good month, or dip toward $1,100 with bad luck and low skill. The game floors a bad month at a $500 loss, so a venture can genuinely cost you money. When one fails, its purchase cost is written off, your happiness drops, stress jumps and credit falls by 6 points — then the bank offers a fresh-start loan. Failure has a consequence and a recovery path, which is the point.

The four quadrants, mapped to the game

The business system is one quadrant of a larger idea. MY LIFE separates where money comes from into four positions, and every choice in the city belongs to one of them:

QuadrantWhat it meansIn MY LIFE
E — EmployeeYou trade hours for a salary; it stops when you stop.Jobs at offices and shops; the first life goal is literally "land your first job (E quadrant)".
S — Self-employedIf you stop, the income stops — but you own the work.Side gigs and odd jobs you take between buildings, paid per task.
B — Business ownerSystems make money without your daily presence.The 11 ventures above; the VENTURE LAB district is the B quadrant in one place.
I — InvestorAssets pay you; money works instead of you.Dividend stocks and rental property — see the stock market simulator.

The game's arc is a move from E toward B and I: a job funds the first asset, assets fund a venture, and a mature venture plus a dividend portfolio eventually covers the bills. A car, by contrast, is deliberately a liability — it never pays you and bills you every month. Learning to tell those two apart is the whole cash-flow lesson, written into the ledger rather than a textbook.

Build a business that runs without you

Owning a job that pays only while you work is a different life from owning a system that pays in your sleep. That distinction is the heart of this game: one of the 19 life goals is literally "own a business that runs without you." Reaching it means your ventures clear enough profit to cover your bills — the same condition the game uses to call it financial freedom. There is no fake win screen; you have to make the numbers work.

Why a business sim inside a life sim

Pure tycoon games isolate the company. MY LIFE keeps the rest of the person in the picture: stress, health, mood, rent, and a city you walk through between decisions. A drained operator runs a worse business; a calm, skilled one runs a better one. That coupling teaches something a spreadsheet cannot — the business is part of a life, not separate from it.

Frequently asked

What is a business simulator game? A game where you start or buy a company, manage its income and costs, and grow it until it produces money without your constant attention. The goal is usually financial freedom, not the highest score.

Does MY LIFE let you own a business? Yes. Alongside jobs, stocks and property, you can build ventures that clear a profit on their own. Owning a business that runs without you is one of the life goals, and it feeds the same cash-flow loop as the rest of the game.

Is it free and browser-based? Yes. It runs in any browser with no download, no account, and no in-game ads. Your progress stays in your own browser.
