Business Simulator Game

A good business simulator game is not about clicking "hire" until a number goes up — it is about the moment a company stops needing you. In MY LIFE, you start at 18 with a little cash and a city full of choices: take a job, learn a skill, buy assets, and eventually build ventures that pay you whether or not you show up. It is a free browser business simulator that folds entrepreneurship into a whole life, with no download and no account.

Build a business that runs without you

Owning a job that pays only while you work is a different life from owning a system that pays in your sleep. That distinction is the heart of this game: one of the 19 life goals is literally "own a business that runs without you." Reaching it means your ventures clear enough profit to cover your bills — the same condition the game uses to call it financial freedom. There is no fake win screen; you have to make the numbers work.

Why a business sim inside a life sim

Pure tycoon games isolate the company. MY LIFE keeps the rest of the person in the picture: stress, health, mood, rent, and a city you walk through between decisions. A drained operator runs a worse business; a calm, skilled one runs a better one. That coupling teaches something a spreadsheet cannot — the business is part of a life, not separate from it.

Frequently asked

What is a business simulator game? A game where you start or buy a company, manage its income and costs, and grow it until it produces money without your constant attention. The goal is usually financial freedom, not the highest score.

Does MY LIFE let you own a business? Yes. Alongside jobs, stocks and property, you can build ventures that clear a profit on their own. Owning a business that runs without you is one of the life goals, and it feeds the same cash-flow loop as the rest of the game.

Is it free and browser-based? Yes. It runs in any browser with no download, no account, and no in-game ads. Your progress stays in your own browser.
